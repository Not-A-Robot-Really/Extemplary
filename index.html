Ballot feedback storage

Every round you ever record — video, transcript, full ballot, and score — lives permanently in your account. My History pulls it all together: a Coach's Overall Notes summary, per-category trend bars, score sparkline graphs, your active goals, and your full round-by-round archive, all in one place.

Streak & calendar

A day-by-day practice streak — recording a ballot, setting a goal, or completing one all keep it alive — shown live via a flame counter in the header and a full monthly calendar. The same calendar tracks your upcoming tournaments and events, so you always know what you're prepping for.

Goals system

Set your own targets — a streak length, an overall score, a threshold in one rubric category, a number of practice rounds or live video ballots this month, or just showing up to a tournament — and watch live progress bars track them. My History also surfaces Suggested Goals, generated automatically from your own weakest categories and recent scores.
